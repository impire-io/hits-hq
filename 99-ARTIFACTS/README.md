# 99-ARTIFACTS

Where research that concluded without becoming a design lands: surveys, comparisons, evaluations, reference documents — knowledge worth keeping that is not a commitment to build anything.

## Rules

- An artifact arrives here through [playbook 02](../00-META/process/02-graduation.md): the research effort closes `concluded-artifact` with `became:` pointing at the document here.
- Artifacts are reference material, never contracts. Nothing downstream may treat an artifact as a decision — anything platform-relevant that was settled along the way gets its own record in [`03-DECISIONS`](../03-DECISIONS/).
- Nothing is deleted; a stale artifact gets a banner line saying what superseded it.
